# Smart guides, relations and dimensions

## Smart guides

While drawing, BurnCraft snaps to endpoints, midpoints, centres, quadrants, 45° points, crossings and the grid, within 10 pixels of an object. Green guides show when a point lines up horizontally or vertically with the previous point or with a point you rested on, or runs parallel or perpendicular to a line you rested on. Turn snapping on or off with **Smart guides** and **Grid snap** in the bottom bar.

## Relations

Relations keep shapes tied together, like in Onshape, SolidWorks or Fusion:

- Snaps used while drawing a line become relations automatically (horizontal, vertical, coincident, midpoint, parallel, perpendicular, on a line or circle).
- With the **Relations** tool (Sketch > Constrain), click one or two items — nodes, lines or circles — and pick a relation: Horizontal, Vertical, Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular, Equal, Midpoint, Tangent, Concentric.
- Relation tags appear next to the items; with the Relations tool, click a tag to remove it.
- Making two loose ends coincident joins them into one continuous path.

If relations can't all be met, nothing is moved and you get a note — remove one of the conflicting relations.

## Dimensions

The **Dimension** tool (D) shows lengths, arc and fillet radii, diameters, corner angles (for the selected shape), star radii and offset distances.

- Click a value and type a new one; Enter applies it. A value you type becomes a **driving** dimension and is held from then on; values in brackets are **driven** (they only report the size).
- Use **Make driven** in the value box to release a driving dimension.
- Changing a dimension keeps every relation and the other driving dimensions — the rest of the sketch adjusts as little as possible.
