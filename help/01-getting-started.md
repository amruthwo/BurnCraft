# Getting started

BurnCraft designs, traces and burns laser jobs, and opens LightBurn projects (.lbrn2 and .lbrn).

## The window

- **Ribbon** (top): commands grouped by task — Home, Sketch, Modify, Arrange, Image, Laser, View.
- **Quick-access bar** (top middle): drag any ribbon button up here to pin it. Right-click a button to pin or unpin.
- **Shapes list** (left): every shape in the project. The top of the list burns first. To rename a shape, right-click it and choose **Rename…**, or click a selected name again and wait a moment. Enter keeps the name; Escape cancels.
- **Canvas** (middle): the laser bed. The origin (0, 0) is the front-left corner.
- **Side panels** (right): Layers, Properties of the selection, and Laser (connect, move, start).
- **Bottom bar**: the colour palette (one colour per layer), Smart guides and Grid snap switches, zoom and cursor position.
- **Project tabs** (very bottom): several projects open at once. Right-click a tab to rename, duplicate or close it.

## Your first job

1. Pick a colour in the palette, then draw with **Rectangle**, **Ellipse**, **Line** or **Text** (Home or Sketch tab).
2. In **Layers**, set the layer's mode (Line, Fill, Image), speed and power.
3. Press **Preview** to see the path the laser will take and how long it will take.
4. **Start** sends the job to a connected laser, or **Save G-code** writes a file.

## Moving around

- Scroll to zoom at the cursor. Drag with the middle or right mouse button to pan.
- Shift + middle-drag zooms. Press the zoom buttons in the View tab to fit the bed or the selection.
- On a touch screen: pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan.

## Finding commands

Press **S** (or Ctrl+K) and type to find any command. Press **F1** for this help, or replay the **Welcome Tour** from Settings.
